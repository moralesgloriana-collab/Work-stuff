01 · Core pattern

Every name has three parts

Underscores separate the parts. Hyphens join the words inside a part. Hover or click a part to see what it carries.

__

Prefix

What the item is. btn_ button, txt_ text frame, anim_ animation, p_ paragraph style.

Descriptor

What it does or shows, by meaning not position. nav-next, hero-team, faq-answer-03.

Variant

Where it is or which one. Page p12, section sec-faq, order 01, language es.

01 · Core rules

Eight rules that apply everywhere

01lowercase for objects, styles, buttons, animations. UPPERCASE only for layers.
02Only a–z 0–9 _ -. No spaces, accents or symbols.
03Start with a letter. Numbered layers are the only exception.
04Zero-pad numbers: 01…10. Pages p01, spreads s01.
05Describe meaning, not position: img_hero-team, not img_top-left.
06No temporary words: final, new, copy, test.
07Language last: _en, _es.
08Under 30 characters.
